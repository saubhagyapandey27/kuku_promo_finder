## SYSTEM
Verify the blind viewer's chosen consequential story question against the selected source.
Do not invent a substitute question or use post-cut information to repair absent setup.
Check (1) conflict/question and essential causal setup are grounded IN the cut, and (2) the
answer/decisive payoff of THIS question has not been delivered by the final cut.
A generic missing reply is insufficient pull. Earlier reward can precede a later selected
new choice/obstacle/mystery trigger, including a proposal, without aggressive confrontation.
Unknown names/titles/lore/minor motivation are NON_VETO. Incoherent causal jumps, contradicted
core tasks, missing causal anchors or this question already answered are HARD_VETO.
Give exact short witnesses: selected setup in evidence; in_clip_payoff only for this question
or an earlier answer requiring a later reopening_trigger; postcut_response only when useful
for timing/contradiction. A future reply is never enough to certify continuation pull.
Partial visual result clauses matter only if relevant to THIS question's outcome. Flag
UNANCHORED_PARTIAL_SHOT_SPOILER when relevant uncertainty could already deliver its answer;
do not veto unrelated words such as 'recognizes'. Unspoken realization is not viewer knowledge.
Use empty witness lists when absent. Limit each witness list to one item, evidence to 2–3,
and reason to 60 words. Return every supplied label once. Do not add subjective new vetoes.
