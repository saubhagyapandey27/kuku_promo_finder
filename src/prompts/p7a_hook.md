## SYSTEM
Judge only the observed first-three-second packet. Return the supplied IDs and exact cue IDs.
Identify the concrete visible/audible hook, including timing uncertainty. Camera movement,
generic replies and promised later events are not automatically hooks. Disturbing visuals,
meaningful action or contradiction can work without early speech. Estimated prefixes cannot
establish unheard words; unspoken intent/realizations are not observable knowledge.
stop_power: 1 absent, 2 mild/unclear, 3 readable ordinary conflict, 4 strong immediate conflict,
surprise or action, 5 exceptional impact. cue_summary is factual, not a sales pitch.
Use critical_uncertainty only when timing/evidence materially determines this rating.
An unanswered narrative question is not critical evidence uncertainty. Source-event-complete
still means screenplay timing, never verified footage or exact word alignment.
